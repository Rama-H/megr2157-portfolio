# Portfolio Overview

Welcome to my engineering portfolio! My name is Rama Hamid, and I am a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, pursuing a concentration in Biomedical Engineering.

This portfolio documents my growth and development as an engineering student through my coursework, projects, research, and professional experiences. It highlights the engineering skills I have developed in areas such as mechanics, thermodynamics, solid mechanics, computational methods, electrical and mechatronics systems, and biomedical engineering.

My interests are focused on the intersection of engineering and healthcare. I am particularly interested in biomedical engineering, medical technology, biomechanics, biomaterials, and clinical applications of engineering. Through research, teaching, coursework, and future internship opportunities, I hope to gain the technical and practical experience necessary to contribute to the development of technologies that improve patient care.

Throughout this portfolio, I will document not only my final results, but also my engineering process—how I define problems, make assumptions, analyze systems, evaluate solutions, and communicate my decisions. My goal is to demonstrate how I am developing into an engineer who can apply technical knowledge to real-world problems and make engineering decisions that I can defend.

As I progress through my degree, I will continue updating this portfolio with new projects, experiences, and accomplishments.
